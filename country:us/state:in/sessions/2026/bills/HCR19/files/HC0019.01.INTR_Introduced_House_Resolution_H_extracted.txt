1 A CONCURRENT RESOLUTION celebrating the 40th

3 Whereas, Since its founding in 1986, the Indianapolis
4 Children's Choir has served more than 2,500 young people
5 each year through high-quality music education programs,
6 fostering artistic excellence, discipline, and personal growth
7 for children ages 18 months through 18 years;
8 Whereas, The Indianapolis Children's Choir draws
9 participants from nearly 20 counties across central Indiana,
10 representing hundreds of schools and school districts, and it
11 offers a wide range of choirs and educational pathways that
12 serve children of all abilities, including early childhood,
13 foundational, preparatory, performing, and special-needs
14 ensembles;
15 Whereas, The Indianapolis Children's Choir has earned
16 national and international recognition for artistic distinction

20 broad catalog of recordings and broadcasts;
