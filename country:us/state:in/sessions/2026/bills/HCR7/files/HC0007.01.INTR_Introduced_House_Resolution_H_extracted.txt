1 A CONCURRENT RESOLUTION congratulating the New
2 Palestine High School football team on winning the 2025
3 Indiana High School Athletic Association Class 5A state
4 championship.
5 Whereas, On November 28, 2025, the New Palestine High
6 School football team concluded its season at Lucas Oil Stadium
7 in Indianapolis, Indiana, securing its fifth Indiana High School
8 Athletic Association (IHSAA);
9 Whereas, Under the leadership of Head Coach Kyle Ralph,
10 with a perfect season record of 14-0, and with 28 consecutive
11 wins, the New Palestine Dragons, ranked first overall in Class
12 5A, advanced to the state championship game against
13 Merrillville High School;
14 Whereas, New Palestine Dragons earned their place in the
15 state championship by defeating Plainfield High School,
16 Cathedral High School, East Central High School, and
17 Bloomington High School South;
18 Whereas, Junior Josh Ranes proved instrumental to New
19 Palestine’s success, recording four tackles half a sack, an
20 interception, 48 yards rushing, and scoring two touchdowns,
21 including a critical 24-yard run on third-and-nine to take a 23-
22 17 lead in the 4th quarter;
23 Whereas, Junior Jacob Davis provided a remarkable
24 performance, rushing for 129 yards on 17 carries and
25 completing six of 11 passes for 84 yards, despite sustaining
26 shoulder and ankle injuries during the contest and continuing
