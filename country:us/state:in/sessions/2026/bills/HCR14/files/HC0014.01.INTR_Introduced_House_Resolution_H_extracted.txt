1 A CONCURRENT RESOLUTION recognizing the Indiana
2 University Hoosiers football team for winning the 2026 College
3 Football Playoff National Championship and for achieving an
4 undefeated season.
5 Whereas, The Indiana University Hoosiers defeated the
6 University of Miami Hurricanes 27-21 to win the College
7 Football Playoff National Championship on January 19, 2026;
8 Whereas, The championship victory capped off a perfect 16-0
9 season for Indiana, marking the best record in the football
10 program's history. Notably, their season included a win over
11 Ohio State University to secure the Big Ten Championship;
12 Whereas, Indiana University's unbeaten run was also a
13 milestone in college football, delivering the first 16-0 season in
14 131 years, tying the record set by the 1894 Yale University
15 football team;
16 Whereas, The championship game against Miami was fiercely
17 contested, with Indiana establishing 10-point leads on three
18 occasions, only for the Hurricanes to narrow the gap each time.
19 However, the Hoosiers never trailed in any of their three
20 playoff games and consistently maintained their lead
21 throughout;
22 Whereas, The entire Indiana University football team rose to
23 the occasion and delivered when their team and the Hoosier
24 fans needed them most, including a spectacular fourth-quarter
25 touchdown run by Heisman Trophy winner Fernando Mendoza;
