1 A HOUSE RESOLUTION recognizing the seriousness of
2 the U.S. national debt and its threat to national security.
3 Whereas, In December 2025, the outstanding public debt
4 of the United States totaled more than $38 trillion, with public
5 debt equaling 118 percent of gross domestic product, and the
6 debt owed per citizens rising $112,000;
7 Whereas, More than $8 trillion of the federal debt is owned
8 by individuals outside the United States, including more than
9 $800 billion owned by individuals in China;

1 Whereas, In fiscal year 2024, the U.S. had a deficit of over
2 $1 trillion after the U.S. Congress failed to pass a balanced
3 budget, and federal tax receipts totaled $5 trillion, short of the
4 federal outlays totaling $6.75 trillion;
5 Whereas, The Social Security and Medicaid board of
6 trustees projects that the Old Age and Survivors Insurance
7 Trust Fund and the Federal Hospital Insurance Trust Fund will
8 be depleted by 2033 and 2036, respectively;
9 Whereas, On September 22, 2011, the Chairman of the
10 Joints Chiefs of Staff, Michael Mullen, warned, "the single,
11 biggest threat to our national security is debt";
12 Whereas, On March 6, 2018, the Director of National
13 Intelligence, Dan Coats, declared, "our continued plunge into
14 debt is unsustainable and represents a dire future threat to our
15 economy and to our national security"; and
16 Whereas, The last federal budget surplus occurred in 2001,
17 and the continued rise in the national debt is a significant
18 concern across Indiana and the around the country: Therefore,
19 Be it resolved by the House of Representatives of the
20 General Assembly of the State of Indiana:
21 SECTION 1. That the State of Indiana recognizes that the
22 national debt is a threat to national security of the United States
23 and urges the United States Congress to commit to establishing
24 an effective regular order for budgeting.
25 SECTION 2. That the Principal Clerk of the House of
26 Representatives shall transmit a copy of this resolution to each
27 member of Indiana's Congressional delegation.
