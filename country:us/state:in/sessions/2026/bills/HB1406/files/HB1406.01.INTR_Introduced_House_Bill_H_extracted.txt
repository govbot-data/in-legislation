HOUSE BILL No. 1406
_____
DIGEST OF INTRODUCED BILL
Citations Affected: IC 6-1.1-22-8.1.
Synopsis: Property tax billing statements. Provides, in a county that
uses a property tax statement as the notice of assessment, that the
county treasurer must send a property tax statement to all property
owners regardless of whether the property has any liability.
Effective: July 1, 2026.
Thompson
January 8, 2026, read first time and referred to Committee on Ways and Means.
