SENATE BILL No. 193
_____
DIGEST OF INTRODUCED BILL
Citations Affected: IC 34-6-2.1-64; IC 34-13-3.
Synopsis: Governmental entity limited liability. Provides that a
governmental entity operating an activity on land leased by the
governmental entity from the federal government is entitled to certain
immunities from a tort claim. Expands the definition of "extreme sport
area" to include an obstacle course.
Effective: July 1, 2026.
Walker G
January 6, 2026, read first time and referred to Committee on Judiciary.
