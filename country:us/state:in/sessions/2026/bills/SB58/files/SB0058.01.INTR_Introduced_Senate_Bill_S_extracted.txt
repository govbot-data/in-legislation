SENATE BILL No. 58
_____
DIGEST OF INTRODUCED BILL
Citations Affected: IC 20-30-5.
Synopsis: School curriculum. Requires each school corporation,
charter school, and state accredited nonpublic elementary school to
include print handwriting, cursive handwriting, and spelling in its
curriculum.
Effective: July 1, 2026.
Leising
December 8, 2025, read first time and referred to Committee on Education and Career
Development.
