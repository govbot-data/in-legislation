1 A SENATE RESOLUTION to raise awareness of title

3
4 Whereas, Title fraud is a serious crime that is often
5 overlooked and results in a property owner losing the title of
6 their property;
7 Whereas, Title fraud, also known as "deed theft," is when
8 the title or deed of a property is transferred illegally, often
9 without the owner's knowledge, using forged documents or fake
10 identification;
11 Whereas, If left unaddressed, title fraud can result in
12 foreclosure or eviction, even if the owner still lives in the home;
13 Whereas, Property owners can help protect their property
14 from fraud by signing up with local government programs
15 involving fraud alerts, periodically checking online tax and
16 property documents, and keeping ownership records secure;
17 Whereas, Warning signs of property fraud include not
18 receiving property tax forms or other bills, receiving mail with
19 another's name on it, and receiving unexpected notices from
