DIGEST OF SB 157 (Updated January 22, 2026 2:55 pm - DI 154)
Citations Affected: IC 27-1.
Synopsis: Disclosures related to prescription drugs. Requires certain
pharmacists or pharmacies to provide the amount of the national
average drug acquisition cost for a generic drug on the written
materials provided to an individual at the point of sale of the generic
drug.
Effective: July 1, 2026.

SENATE BILL No. 157
A BILL FOR AN ACT to amend the Indiana Code concerning
insurance.
Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Indiana:
1 SECTION 1. IC 27-1-24.6-6, AS ADDED BY P.L.216-2025,
2 SECTION 41, IS AMENDED TO READ AS FOLLOWS [EFFECTIVE
3 JULY 1, 2026]: Sec. 6. (a) A health plan must provide the amount of
4 the national average drug acquisition cost for a generic drug to the plan
5 sponsor.
6 (b) This subsection does not apply to a hospital licensed under
7 IC 16-21-2, a federally qualified health center (as defined in 42
8 U.S.C. 1396d(l)(2)(B)), or a health facility licensed under IC 16-28.
9 A pharmacist (as defined in IC 27-1-24.5-9) or pharmacy (as
10 defined in IC 27-1-24.5-11) must provide the amount of the
11 national average drug acquisition cost for a generic drug on the
12 written materials provided to an individual at the point of sale of
13 the generic drug.
